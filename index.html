Services

Safety and claims, managed as one program

Every injury becomes a claim, and every open claim costs you money in reserves, premium and captive returns. I work both sides so the jobsite and the loss run improve together.

On-site safety audits

Walkthroughs of active jobsites, yards and shops with a written findings report, a rating for each item and a named owner for every correction.

OSHA compliance

Written programs, citation response and inspection readiness for Cal/OSHA and Nevada OSHA, including fall protection, silica, rigging and heat illness.

Claims management & TPA oversight

Claim reviews with your adjusters, reserve challenges, closure plans and litigation strategy. Your TPA executes; you set the direction.

EMR analysis & reduction

I model how each open claim drives your experience mod, then target the ones that move it most before the next rating date.

Captive & loss fund strategy

Frequency fund and large loss fund analysis for group captive members, with a plan to protect refunds and strengthen renewal terms.

Loss prevention analytics

Loss runs broken down by entity, division, injury type and hours worked, so you see the 20% of exposures behind 80% of losses.

Training & toolbox talks

Crew-level training built around your actual injury trends, plus foreman coaching that sticks after I leave.

Return-to-work programs

Modified-duty programs that bring injured workers back sooner, shorten lost time and keep indemnity dollars off your mod.

Retained advisory

Ongoing access for incident calls, claim questions and leadership reporting, on a monthly retainer sized to your operation.

Approach

Lead with the finding, back it with the number

Findings are specific: the entity, the division, the claim and the dollar figure. Recommendations are one sentence with an owner and a date.

  1. 01

    Assess

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Loss runs, hours worked and EMR worksheets broken down to find where injuries and dollars concentrate.

  3. 03

    Act

    Targeted controls in the field and closure plans for open claims, prioritized by cost and EMR impact.

  4. 04

    Advise

    Ongoing tracking and plain-language reporting to ownership, brokers and captive boards.

About the firm

An independent advisor who has walked the jobsite and read the loss run

West Consulting LLC is an independent safety and claims management consultancy. I am not paid by a carrier, broker or TPA, so my only job is lowering your cost of risk.

I work with contractors on the ground in California and Nevada and understand the differences that matter between the two: separate OSHA programs, separate workers' compensation systems and separate experience mods.

Rick, Principal, West Consulting LLC
